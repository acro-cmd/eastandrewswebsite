STAIR: St. Andrews Impact Research

About STAIR

Inspired by similar projects across other world leading universities, The St. Andrews Impact Research project is a 6 week program for students to explore a research project in a pressing global issue under mentorship. While research experience is valued, no prior research experience is needed!

    STAIR Student Researcher Role

    STAIR allows student researchers to:

    • gain research experience and mentorship
    • connect with other bright students who share your academic interests
    • explore research topics you believe are important
    • create positive social impact by contributing to research that can help others
    • develop research skills and explore a direction you might not otherwise have the capacity to pursue

    What role do student researchers serve?

    1. Exploring Research Ideas
    2. Refining Research Ideas
    3. Taking on Project Leadership
    4. Attending the Kickoff and Pitching Research Topics
    5. Working with Your Research Team
    6. Providing and Receiving Critical Feedback

    Total time commitment

    • ~3hrs developing and refining a research topic
    • 1-2 hrs every week for 8 weeks (conducting research, collaborating with your team etc)
    • Total = 19 hrs (~10 weeks)

    STAIR Mentor Role

    STAIR allows mentors to:

    • gain research oversight and mentorship experience
    • connect with bright students who share your academic interests
    • promote topics you believe are important
    • create positive social impact by training the next generation of contributors and leaders in your field
    • peek into a research direction you would otherwise not have the capacity to explore

    What role do mentors serve?

    1. Proposing Research Ideas
    2. Refining Research Ideas
    3. Selecting project leads
    4. Attending the Kickoff and Pitching Research Topics
    5. Providing Ongoing Mentorship
    6. Supplying Critical Feedback

    Total time commitment

    • ~3hrs developing and refining a research topic
    • 1-2 hrs every week for 8 weeks (Attending group meetings, guiding research, appointing a leader etc)
    • Total = 19 hrs (~10 weeks)
